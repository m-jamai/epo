How I approach a project

The V-model discipline used on automotive programs, scaled to the size of the problem.

  1. 1

    Frame

    Pin down the system, its requirements and what “done” means before any model exists.

  2. 2

    Model

    Build plant and controller models with their assumptions written down, at the fidelity the decision needs.

  3. 3

    Validate

    Close the loop in MiL, SiL and HiL, with traceable evidence against each requirement.

  4. 4

    Hand over

    Deliver documented models, test reports and a walkthrough, so your team owns what was built.
